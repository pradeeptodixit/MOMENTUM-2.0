## 🎯 What is Momentum?

Traditional productivity software assumes:
$$\text{User} \longrightarrow \text{Plan} \longrightarrow \text{Execute}$$

Real human life operates with chaotic interruptions, fluctuating energy, and shifting time. In traditional apps, when life happens, the plan breaks, streaks die, users feel shame, and they abandon the tool.

**Momentum operates on a completely different premise:**
$$\text{User} \longrightarrow \text{Understand Reality} \longrightarrow \text{Compile Goal} \longrightarrow \text{Today's Mission} \longrightarrow \text{Execute} \longrightarrow \text{Observe Friction} \longrightarrow \text{Adapt Continuously} \longrightarrow \text{Grow}$$

*“The perfect plan is useless if it doesn’t survive real life.”*

## ✨ 5 Hero "WOW" Moments for Judges

1. **The Goal Compiler (Instant Deconstruction)**:
   Enter any vague aspiration (*"Crack my coding interview in 30 days"*, *"Learn Python"*, *"Launch an MVP"*). In seconds, Momentum transforms it into structured milestones, dependencies, and compresses it into **"What Matters Now"**—a single 25-minute mission with **The One Thing** isolated.

2. **The Dynamic Mission Auto-Compressor**:
   Got 60 minutes planned, but suddenly your schedule collapses? Select **10m** or **5m**. Momentum immediately auto-compresses the session without guilt, shaming, or backlog accumulation, fiercely protecting your highest-leverage task.

3. **1-Tap Friction Radar**:
   Instead of recording "task skipped" and inducing guilt, tap **"Friction?"** in 1 tap. Tell Momentum *why* (*"Too difficult"*, *"Takes too long today"*, *"Lost motivation"*). The AI immediately transforms the intimidating task into a 3-minute visual primer or bite-sized micro-steps.

4. **Zero-Shame Momentum Reset (Recovery Mode)**:
   Missed days due to illness or heavy work? Traditional apps say *"Streak Broken"*. Momentum says: **"Your plan stopped fitting your week. What changed?"** You pick what happened, and Momentum rebuilds tomorrow's mission clean from where you actually are.

5. **Weekly "What Momentum Learned About You"**:
   Data-grounded retrospectives showing empirical patterns (*"You complete 42% more tasks when missions are under 35 minutes"*, *"Evening flow window has 0 skips"*), followed by concrete system adaptations for the coming week.
