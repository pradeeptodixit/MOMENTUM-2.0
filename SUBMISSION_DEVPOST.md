## 💡 Inspiration
Every year, millions of people set ambitious goals: *"I'll learn to code in 3 months,"* *"I'll run a 10K,"* *"I'll launch a startup."* They download to-do lists, habit trackers, or project management boards. 

Within two weeks, real life strikes: a late shift at work, unexpected sickness, or burnout. The static app responds with guilt: **"STREAK BROKEN."** 

Faced with an overwhelming backlog of past-due tasks and psychological shame, users abandon the app. 

We asked ourselves:
> **Why do productivity tools expect humans to adapt to inflexible plans, instead of building plans that adapt to real human lives?**

This is why we built **MOMENTUM AI 2.0**: The Adaptive Human Execution Engine.

---

## ⚡ What It Does

Momentum is not another to-do list, calendar, habit tracker, or AI wrapper. It is an **adaptive execution engine**.

1. **Goal Compiler**: Takes any unstructured ambition (*"Crack my coding interview in 30 days"*) and decomposes it into milestones, dependencies, and compresses it into **"What Matters Now"**—a daily 25-minute mission protecting **The One Thing**.
2. **Dynamic Auto-Compression**: If your available time drops from 60m to 10m or 5m, Momentum auto-compresses the mission in real-time, preserving the highest-leverage action without guilt or backlog accumulation.
3. **Friction Radar**: When you're stuck or skip an action, 1 tap tells Momentum *why* (*"Too difficult"*, *"Too long"*, *"Low energy"*). Momentum immediately restructures the obstacle into a 3-minute visual primer or bite-sized micro-steps.
4. **Zero-Shame Recovery (Momentum Reset)**: If you miss several days, Momentum never shames you. It asks: *"Your plan stopped fitting your week. What changed?"* and re-anchors your trajectory from where you are today.
5. **Reality Twin**: Compares your *Intended Capacity* (what you wish you could do) with your *Observed Capacity* (what you actually execute), identifying your natural sweet spot (e.g. 35m) and peak execution windows.
6. **Weekly Momentum Reviews**: Extracts empirical execution insights (*"You complete 42% more tasks when missions are under 35 minutes"*) and adapts next week's schedule automatically.
7. **Viral Momentum Proof Cards & 7-Day Challenges**: Tangible evidence of verified consistency that users can share with deep links to challenge their peers.
